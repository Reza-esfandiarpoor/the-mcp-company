On Gitlab, under bustub project (repo name: 'root/bustub'), there is an issue titled "Implement HyperLogLog Algorithm". 
In this issue, there is instruction about a project called HyperLogLog algorithm. 

You need to copy the project instruction from gitlab to your company wiki page.
Your company wiki page is in the wiki section of the documentation repo on gitlab (documentation gitlab repo name: 'root/doc').
Create a new wiki page with title HyperLogLog_project and let Gitlab auto-generate the wiki URL, and return the url of that wiki page. Write that as a .txt file called wiki_url.txt under the /workspace directory.
